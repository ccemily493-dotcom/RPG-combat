# Universal RPG Engine v0.1.1 — Calibration Completion Report

## Delivered

- 48 specialized benchmark profiles: eight specializations across all six categories, generated from declared within-category placements.
- Accuracy margin, threshold-proximity, per-profile, and independent-stat sensitivity diagnostics.
- Canonical defense-stage instrumentation plus three declarative composition models stored outside engine code.
- Damage sensitivity across equal, one-category, two-category, and extreme gaps with balanced and specialized profiles.
- Deterministic uniform, centered-triangular, and bounded-normal-like micro-variance distributions, all restricted to 0.99–1.01; uniform remains canonical.
- Authorized immutable tuning overlays and generic 1D/2D parameter sweeps with JSON, CSV, seeded replay, and suspicious-outcome ranking.
- Expanded performance diagnostics and new calibration infrastructure tests.

No Ability Parser, universe extension, multi-attack execution, optimizer, or automatic rebalancing was introduced.

## Verification

- 51 automated tests passed; 0 failed.
- All 25 hard invariants remain separate from balance diagnostics.
- 21,440 seeded calibration encounters were executed, plus 896 seeded parameter-sweep encounters.
- Golden replay matched after the trace schema gained variance sample provenance.
- Canonical balance values were not changed.

## Main evidence

- Equal-category accuracy becomes progressively less favorable to the attacker: balanced margins move from approximately -5 at Civilian parity to -19 at Legendary parity.
- Across specialized defender cohorts, active filtering averages 5.52% under the canonical fully multiplicative model, 27.35% under experimental weighted composition, and 16.59% under experimental grouped composition.
- The largest canonical defense reductions are initial capacity, timing, and execution. Feasibility, full stability, and paid cost cause no collapse in the benchmark fixtures.
- Fighter vs Civilian damage remains approximately 58.79: 64 base power × 83.2% partial-hit exposure produces about 53.23 contact power; only 17.16% is combined-filtered, and the residual is divided by a 0.75 Civilian harm-capacity scale.
- Damage averages are 11.33 for equal-category, 28.79 for one-category, 56.17 for two-category, and 125.74 for extreme-gap specialized cohorts.
- Variance standard deviation is approximately 0.00578 uniform, 0.00408 centered triangular, and 0.00237 bounded normal-like. Bounds remain unchanged.

## Performance

- Throughput: approximately 1,234 encounters/second.
- Median full resolution: 0.7102 ms.
- P95 full resolution: 1.1688 ms.
- Median trace-emission overhead: 0.0045 ms using alternating identical-seed samples.
- Median isolated schema-validation cost: 0.0204 ms, approximately 2.87% of median full resolution.

## Human tuning decisions required before v0.2

1. Decide whether equal-tier hit margins should decline as category level rises, or whether accuracy/evasion weight totals should be normalized.
2. Define desired active-defense ranges, then choose whether to keep multiplicative composition or promote a weighted/grouped experiment.
3. Define acceptable damage ranges for equal, one-category, two-category, and extreme gaps, including glass-cannon and defense-specialist tails.
4. Decide whether sub-100 harm-capacity scaling should amplify damage as strongly as it currently does for Civilian targets.
5. Choose the canonical micro-variance distribution: uniform, centered triangular, or bounded normal-like.
6. Decide which balance expectations should become non-blocking regression warnings; do not promote them to hard invariants without an explicit policy decision.
