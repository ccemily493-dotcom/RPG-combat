# Universal RPG Engine v0.6 + v0.6.1 + v0.7 — Completion Report

## 1. Completion status

All three sequential gates passed. v0.6 completed before v0.6.1, and v0.6.1 completed before v0.7. The final repository check reports 288 tests passed, zero failed, and all 145 declared hard invariants executable and green.

## 2. Files created

Created `extension-manifest.schema.json`, `src/extension-sdk/*`, `extensions/jjk-reference/*`, extension tests, extension replay/diagnostic CLIs, three extension goldens, two checkpoint reports, performance/diagnostic reports, and this completion report.

## 3. Files changed

Updated `package.json`, `README.md`, `open-decisions.md`, `invariants.yaml`, `cli/validate.js`, `test/invariants.test.js`, and the existing turn/session/ability/semantic goldens whose configuration hashes changed when invariants were promoted.

## 4. Dependency architecture

The implemented direction is `Extension package → Ability Parser/Semantic Input → normalized contracts → RPG Engine`. The engine core does not import the Extension SDK, Ability Parser, Semantic Input, or JJK content.

## 5. Manifest schema

The Draft 2020-12 manifest schema covers ID, namespace, version, SDK/ruleset compatibility, dependencies, declared data files, and non-mechanical metadata.

## 6. Declarative package loader

The loader reads only declared JSON/YAML files beneath the package root, rejects path traversal, validates before composition, and never executes extension-provided JavaScript.

## 7. Dependency resolution

Dependencies are topologically ordered by stable extension ID. Missing dependencies, incompatible versions, and cycles raise structured extension errors before compilation.

## 8. Namespace isolation

Registry IDs, ability IDs, and stat aliases are verified against the manifest namespace. JJK data uses `jjk.*` or `jjk:*` identifiers.

## 9. Registry composition

Resources, statuses, defense sources, and equipment compose deterministically. Duplicate keys fail with `EXTENSION_REGISTRY_COLLISION`; no load-order shadowing exists.

## 10. Immutability and hashes

Compiled packages and registries are deeply immutable. Mechanical and full-provenance hashes are separate; names/descriptions/author metadata do not affect mechanics.

## 11. Scaling profiles

Extension scaling layers over the existing universal 0–100 stats and category hints. It does not replace categories or add unauthorized core stats.

## 12. Cursed-energy resource

`jjk.resource.cursed_energy` is namespaced and handled by ordinary resource accounting. The engine has no special knowledge of its meaning.

## 13. Independent energy stats

Reserve, output, control, and efficiency map respectively to `energy_capacity`, `energy_output`, `energy_control`, and `energy_efficiency`. Sensitivity tests prove independent formula responsibilities.

## 14. Grade profiles

Grades 4–1 and special grade are extension metadata/category hints only; they do not replace engine categories or create a global grade score.

## 15. v0.6 profiles

Five deterministic profiles cover balanced, high-reserve/poor-efficiency, high-control/low-output, physical specialist, and barrier specialist cases.

## 16. v0.6 abilities

Eight synthetic abilities cover raw energy offense, reinforcement, reverse recovery, barrier defense, cursed-tool interaction, conditional contact, a synthetic domain, and a persistent assistant summon.

## 17. Ability authority

Extension abilities produce ordinary actions, status attempts, modifiers, temporal effects, defense sources, opportunities, and Strategy DAG fragments. They never produce final hits, defense, damage, status application, or state.

## 18. Semantic vocabulary

English and Spanish extension dictionaries cover cursed energy, reinforcement, reverse recovery, barriers, domains, vows, summons, and tools. Entries retain extension/ability provenance.

## 19. v0.6 scenarios

The declarative scenario matrix contains A–O coverage for manifests, dependencies, collisions, hashes, stats, profiles, ability compilation, and bilingual semantics. Executable tests cover the associated hard contracts.

## 20. v0.6 checkpoint

The v0.6 checkpoint passed with core-specific JJK changes/imports/branches all equal to zero. See `reports/v0.6-checkpoint-report.md`.

## 21. Barrier architecture

Barriers remain defense sources, world/character modifiers, temporal state, and normalized actions. No barrier formula was added to core engine code.

## 22. Domain composition

The synthetic domain is a composite temporal/world effect with explicit resource/area semantics. It does not bypass ordinary accuracy, defense, penetration, or status resolution.

## 23. No automatic hit

`automatic_hit` is explicitly false in extension configuration, and tests confirm domain declarations contain no final hit authority.

## 24. Domain conflicts

Conflicts compare refinement, stability, output, coverage, timing, compatibility, and environment independently. Results retain every per-property delta and deterministic favored side.

## 25. Domain outcomes

The extension permits `DOMINATE`, `PARTIAL`, `DESTABILIZE`, `COLLAPSE`, `MUTUAL`, and `LOCAL`. The conservative generic resolver currently emits dominate/partial/destabilize/mutual; collapse/local remain explicit extension-policy endpoints.

## 26. No domain score

Conflict output includes `scalar_score: null`. Dominance counts are audit information, not a universal mechanical stat.

## 27. Binding vows

Vows require declared conditions, restrictions, compensation, duration, and violation consequences. The resolver returns normalized consequence attempts and never judges narrative fairness.

## 28. No vow score

`aggregate_score` is false. No compensation-equivalence, vow quality, or automatic power adjustment is inferred.

## 29. Persistent summons

Summon templates create ordinary persistent entities. They have their own stats and economy and do not inherit free actions from an owner.

## 30. Conditional requirements

Supported contracts explicitly cover contact, spoken, heard, single target, range, concentration, prior mark, delay, and resource threshold.

## 31. Conditional cancellation

The reference conditional technique permits one active target; changing/cancelling removes the relation, trigger, and pending consequence as declared data.

## 32. Stateful batching

Status-heavy batching remains individual by default. No generic aggregate approximation was enabled.

## 33. v0.6.1 scenarios

The scenario matrix contains A–O cases for barriers, domain comparisons, no auto-hit, vows, summons, conditional contracts, and status batching.

## 34. v0.6.1 checkpoint

The v0.6.1 checkpoint passed with invalid core leakage equal to zero. See `reports/v0.6.1-checkpoint-report.md`.

## 35. Asura definition

Asura is stored only in `extensions/jjk-reference/content.json`; the core source audit finds no Asura reference or branch.

## 36. Asura stat mapping

Strength 76→physical capability, Speed 56→movement, Resistance 4→resistance, Reflexes 14→reaction, Coordination 3→coordination, Reserve 92→capacity, Output 67→output, Control 94→control, Efficiency 26→efficiency, Perception 7→perception, and Temple 92→strategy.

## 37. Asura resource behavior

The 92-point namespaced reserve is conserved by ordinary action reservation/commit. Output changes attack power; control changes accuracy/mark potency; efficiency remains independently available for cost derivation.

## 38. Asura raw manipulation

Declared capabilities are limited to raw reinforcement, raw output, and the conditional contact contract. No undocumented named or canon-complete technique database was added.

## 39. Asura conditional technique

The technique requires contact, spoken/heard conditions, concentration, and one target. It produces a status/relation attempt; only the engine decides contact and application.

## 40. Strategy integration

The compiled DAG is `DISTRACTION (ACTION) → REPOSITION (MOVEMENT) → contact check (ZERO_TIME) → conditional attempt (ACTION)`. `ON_PARTIAL_OR_BETTER` and `ON_SUCCESS` remain distinct, and economy—not graph depth—determines same-turn versus cross-turn execution.

## 41. Diagnostic comparisons

Asura is compared structurally with all five synthetic profiles. The very low resistance/reaction/coordination/perception and poor efficiency are intentionally suspicious tails; no coefficients were silently changed.

## 42. Synthetic enemy domain

Asura can be evaluated inside the synthetic enemy-domain state through normal world modifiers and multi-property conflict data. There is no special Asura or enemy-domain resolver.

## 43. Twenty-turn campaign
